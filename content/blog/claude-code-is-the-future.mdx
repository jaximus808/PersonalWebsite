A human just knows, and if you own your work, you know instantly where to look. Thats the only saving grace for programming. And saving memory is pretty slow for AI models, it needs to be loaded token by token, and it can rag sure but that is still a bottle neck compared to a human instantly just knowing.

Thats the only saving grace left, our human neurons can encode specific memory easier than an LLM needing to CHUG a bazillion gallons of water to remember in its neurons how to write a C++ lambda, and god forbid navigating your code base.

TLDR: the AI has not crossed the bridge of instantly learning human design. It knows "rules of the universe" but knowing how to use your horrible code, it has no idea and has to grep and tokenize everytime.
