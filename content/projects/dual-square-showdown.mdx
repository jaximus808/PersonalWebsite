A game where you fight an enemy large square that shoots a multitude of projectiles for you to dodge. You need to destroy pillars that spawn to do significant damage to the boss while controlling the direction of your bullets. The first version of the game was made in 2 days and was a way for me to make sure my skills were there to be able to confidently offer my services on fiverr.
